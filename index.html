Upgrade the LC data

One time only · Banking and Cash LC open after this

The new Finance app keeps every LC as its documents (at bank → accepted → paid), so the same fact is stored only once. Your existing records are converted once. First run the check: it shows the old figures, every adjustment, and the new figures. You can convert only when they reconcile to the cent.

  • Nothing is deleted. A copy of the spreadsheet is saved to Drive and the old tabs are archived first.
  • If anything fails, the conversion undoes itself automatically.
  • You can undo it later from Backup → Undo the LC upgrade (PIN).
